Release notes Privacy policy

Open the DMG, drag the app into Applications, then on first launch use right-click > Open or allow it in Privacy & Security if macOS blocks it.

FAQ

Two quick answers.

Does InputStats upload my activity to the cloud?

No. Stats stay on your Mac.

Why does the app request macOS permissions?

Global stats require Accessibility and Input Monitoring.

Does the app record what I type?

No. It only stores key counts and aggregated click regions, not input content.

How will updates work?

Download the latest DMG again and replace the app manually.

What if macOS says the app cannot be opened?

Move the app to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open. If needed, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
